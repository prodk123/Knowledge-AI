# Marketing Department Guidelines

## Marketing Budget
The annual marketing budget is reviewed quarterly. No department may exceed their 
allocated quarterly budget without VP approval. Unused budget does not roll over.

## Campaign Approval
All external advertising campaigns must be reviewed by the Legal department before launch. 
Campaigns with a budget over $10,000 require approval from the Director of Marketing.

## Advertising Expenses
Social media ad spend is capped at $50,000 per month across all platforms. 
Influencer partnerships require a signed standard contract and must clearly disclose 
sponsorship in accordance with FTC guidelines.
