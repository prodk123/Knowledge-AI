# Enterprise Employee Handbook

## Annual Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. 
Leave must be requested at least two weeks in advance through the HR portal. Up to 5 days 
of unused leave can be carried over to the next year.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave annually. A doctor's note is required for 
absences exceeding 3 consecutive days.

## Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week, subject to manager approval. 
Core working hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in the employee's local timezone.
