# Corporate Finance Policy

## Expense Reimbursement
All business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the transaction. 
Receipts are required for any expense over $25. Meals during travel are capped at $75 per day.

## Travel Expenses
Flights should be booked at least 14 days in advance to secure the best rates. 
Only economy class is permitted for domestic flights. Business class is permitted for 
international flights longer than 8 hours. The maximum hotel rate is $250 per night.

## Approval Limits
- Managers: Can approve expenses up to $5,000.
- Directors: Can approve expenses up to $25,000.
- VP/C-Suite: Must approve any expense exceeding $25,000.
