# Company Overview

## Mission Statement
Our mission is to build the most secure and scalable AI solutions for the modern enterprise. 
Founded in 2024, we believe that AI should empower workers, not replace them.

## Departments
1. Engineering: Responsible for building our core RAG platform.
2. Sales & Marketing: Responsible for growth and customer acquisition.
3. Operations: Includes HR, Finance, and Legal.
4. Customer Success: Ensures clients get the maximum value from our products.
